Colordale Support

Need help? Email support@webindexa.com and we'll get back to you.

Where are the settings?

Tap Grown-ups on the home screen and answer the grown-up question. There you can turn the sounds and the voice on or off, set the volume, set a daily play-time limit, adjust color brightness, and delete pictures.

Is Colordale free?

It is free to download, with no ads. 26 pictures are free for good: the first two in every category. The picture of the day is free for everyone on its day too. The other pictures show a small lock and need Coloring Plus, a subscription: $3.99 a month, or $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial (prices in US dollars; yours are shown in the app).

How do I subscribe, and how do I cancel?

Tap a picture with a lock, then I'm a grown-up, and answer the grown-up question; or tap Grown-ups on the home screen, then See Plans. Apple handles the payment through your Apple Account. To cancel, open Grown-ups and tap Manage Subscription, or go to Settings, your name, Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the trial or the period ends to avoid the next charge.

What happens to my child's pictures if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. Pictures of locked drawings stay in My Gallery; to keep coloring them, a drawing has to be free or Coloring Plus active again.

I was charged and want a refund

Apple handles every payment and refund; we can't see or refund charges ourselves. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com, choose the charge and "Request a refund".

I subscribed, but pictures are still locked

Open Grown-ups and tap Restore Purchases, on a device signed in with the Apple Account that subscribed. With Family Sharing, the organizer's purchase sharing must be on. A purchase "waiting for approval" unlocks once the family organizer approves it.

How do I save, share or print a picture?

In My Gallery, tap the … button on a picture, then Share or Print. A grown-up question comes first. Share also offers Save Image to put the picture in Photos.

How do I print coloring pages?

One picture: open any drawing, colored or not, tap More (the three dots), then Print. After the grown-up question you can print the picture as colored, or a blank page to color with crayons.

Many at once: tap Grown-ups on the home screen, then Print coloring pages. Tick whole categories, or tap a category's arrow to pick single pictures, from as many categories as you like, then tap Print. Each picture prints on its own sheet, in black only.

There's no sound

Check that the device isn't on silent and the volume is up. Then open Grown-ups and check that the sound and voice switches are on.

My child saw a "Time for a break!" screen

That's the daily play-time limit. A grown-up can tap I'm a grown-up on that screen to add more time, or change or turn off the limit in Grown-ups.

Where are my child's pictures stored?

Only on the device. They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes them.
