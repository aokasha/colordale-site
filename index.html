A coloring book that comes alive.

Colordale is a coloring app for children ages 3 to 5. Big pictures, bright colors, paint that stays inside the lines, and a friendly shark who cheers them on.

What it does

Made so every picture turns out well

Nothing to read and nothing to get wrong. A child taps a color, touches the picture, and it works.

🖍️

Paint stays inside the lines

Tap an area to fill it, or scribble with a crayon. The color stays in the part of the picture it started in.

✨

Pictures come alive

Finish a picture and it moves: fish swim, rabbits hop, rockets fly, with confetti and a cheer.

⭐

Stickers to earn

Every finished picture becomes a sticker in the album. Finish a whole category to earn its trophy.

🔤

Learning while coloring

A fun fact read aloud for every picture, color names spoken as they're picked, letters, numbers, and color by number.

🪄

Tools for little hands

Fill, crayon and eraser in three sizes, 58 colors, 10 magic brushes such as glitter and rainbow, and a blank page for drawing anything.

🖼️

A gallery of their own

Pictures save by themselves. Finished ones hang in a frame, and an art show plays them one after another for the family.

For grown-ups

Safe to hand over

Colordale was built for the App Store's Kids Category, for ages 5 and under. It asks for nothing and sends nothing.

Read the privacy policy

  • No ads and no trackingColordale collects no personal information. There are no accounts.
  • Everything includedAll 192 pictures, every color and every brush are free. There is nothing to buy.
  • Works offlineIn the car, on a plane, anywhere. Pictures stay on the device.
  • A grown-ups area behind a gateSound settings, sharing, saving and printing are behind a question a small child can't answer.
  • A daily play-time limitSet 15 to 60 minutes. A gentle "time for a break" appears when it's used up.
  • Print a page to color on paperAny drawing prints as a blank page, or print the picture your child colored.
  • Made to be accessibleUpright or sideways, light and dark mode, VoiceOver and larger text.
