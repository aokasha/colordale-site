Colordale Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: Colordale collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't connect to our servers. Your child's pictures stay on your device.

Colordale is a coloring app for children ages 3 to 5, made by Webindexa. This page explains what the app keeps on your device, what it never collects, and what a grown-up can choose to do.

What we collect

Nothing. The app never asks for a name, email address, birthday, photo or location, and it has no sign-up. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party code. It doesn't use the camera or the microphone. Nothing about your child, or about how the app is used, is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Pictures your child colors, with their names and the dates they were made and changed.
  • Stickers earned for finished pictures.
  • Settings chosen in the grown-ups area, such as sound, voice and the daily play-time limit.

These are kept only inside the app on your device. Like all app data, they are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, under Apple's privacy policy. A grown-up can delete pictures in the grown-ups area, and deleting the app removes everything.

Sharing, saving and printing

A grown-up can share a picture, save it to Photos or print it. Each of these first asks a grown-up question that young children are unlikely to answer. The picture then goes only where the grown-up sends it, using the device's own share sheet or AirPrint. To save to Photos, the app asks only for permission to add pictures; it can never see or read your photo library.

Purchases

Colordale is currently free, with every picture included. If an optional subscription is offered in the future, it will be available only in the grown-ups area, behind the grown-up question, and handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We would never see your name or payment details.

Links

Links that leave the app, such as this page, appear only in the grown-ups area, behind the grown-up question.

Children's privacy

Colordale is made for children and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and similar laws by not collecting any personal information at all. If you believe information about a child has reached us anyway, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
